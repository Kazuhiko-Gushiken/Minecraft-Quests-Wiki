
&n&lWelcome to The Quest Wiki!

This is where you can find a wiki for a plethora of mods, but in quest format! Everyone loves a set of quests; it helps guide in progression and is easy to follow along. So, why not use the same format for a wiki, where all the information is gathered?

That's the idea, and that's what this is all about. You'll find the mods as well as their addons in the left most sidebar. Clicking on a chapter opens it up. You can pan and zoom throughout the chapter. Toggle the grid in the bottom left. Clicking on a quest opens a screen, which can be directly linked to via the URL or the link copy icon in the top right of the quest dialogue box. Pressing escape or clicking outside of the box will close the quest. Ctrl+Clicking on the quest chapter button will bring up the credits for the chapter, including icon and mod creators.
